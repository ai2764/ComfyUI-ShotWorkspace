# Complete script to shot plan

The exact source_excerpt spans must together cover the complete script, including scene headings and the ending; do not summarize or skip source paragraphs. Adjacent excerpts may overlap when several shots cover one dramatic beat.

Read the selected director knowledge before interpreting the user's script. Plan the complete supplied script in one response, not just its opening scene. Determine shot count from dramatic beats, changes in viewpoint, action and editing purpose. Do not split mechanically by sentence or force every cut to use Motion Context. Each shot is one coherent video-generation unit with achievable action and room for speech, reaction and an ending hold. Preserve story facts; do not add dialogue. Cinematic decisions may interpret staging without changing the story.

Before choosing shots, return a compact source-grounded event record, not a reasoning essay. Separate participants (including who addresses whom), request or stated wish, actual decision, actual observable action, and result. A request/threat/offer does not establish acceptance or execution. Explicit story outcomes override implications guessed from speech. Leave unknown fields empty; retain ambiguity instead of inventing motives or actions. This applies to all languages and stories.

Keep event records short (usually fewer than shots), and link every shot to existing event keys. Choose cuts for changes in audience understanding, action or viewpoint; changing the speaker alone is not a cut reason. A shared master can contain a whole exchange and its listening reaction. Use a closer/reverse view only when it reveals something the previous view cannot. Do not force fewer shots or one long take when geography, information or action genuinely requires a cut. The beat record must agree with the visible brief, dialogue and source. Track what the audience learns even in a still or ambiguous scene.

Before returning, compare every brief and beat against the source event record: preserve who requests, who decides, who moves and the resulting state. Remove unestablished events and unsupported decorative props/weather/light behavior. Author useful framing, eyelines and blocking rather than plot changes. Budget for speech, action and a completed response before the clip ends. These are authored proposals, not verified physical execution.

Return 1–48 shots covering all scenes. Each shot_key is unique. A brief describes visible subjects, place, action, shot size, camera/blocking, story purpose and meaningful sound. Do not write the six-section H3 prompt or Picture tags here. Keep speech in dialogue_lines, exactly once; do not repeat spoken words inside brief. Use [] for no speech. Quotes printed on props/signs are not dialogue. Explicit continuation uses {"mode":"continue","source_shot_key":"earlier shot key"}; cuts/reaction shots can use off. These are planning relationships only. duration_s must be positive and at most 120; choose a realistic short clip, rather than packing a whole scene into five seconds.
