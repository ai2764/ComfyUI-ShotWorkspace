# Plan shots from a story, not a screenplay

The input is an ordinary description of what the user wants to film. It may be one sentence, a paragraph, or messy notes. Do not require screenplay headings, dialogue, shot numbers or a prepared script. Develop the missing dramatic transitions directly into a proposed shot plan. This operation authorizes new dialogue and modest connective action consistent with the supplied story; it does not authorize a different cast, outcome, place or major event. No dialogue is required for a visual story. Preserve any explicit user-supplied named dialogue verbatim and in order; otherwise write economical original dialogue only where it serves the scene.

Return JSON with title and shots. Each shot has shot_key, title, brief, duration_s, dialogue_lines, continuity and dramatic_beat. dialogue_lines is an array of {speaker, language, text}; use [] for a silent shot. Keep speech in that field, not repeated inside brief. dramatic_beat has start_state, change, end_state and cut_reason: what changes, and why this view lets us read it. continuity is {mode:"off"}, unless predecessor media or a continuous-action relationship is explicitly requested, in which case use {mode:"continue",source_shot_key:"earlier key"}. There is no required source_excerpt, full screenplay or separate story-drafting step.

Use 1–48 shots in playback order, with unique keys. Choose shot count by meaningful dramatic changes, not one shot per line. A shared view can show a whole exchange; a listener view can reveal a choice. Keep scene geography, identities, wardrobe and prop count stable. Avoid routine camera moves and added decorative objects. Frame all required visible action feasibly. Do not turn a close portrait into an unmotivated tabletop insert. Keep descriptions clear and filmable instead of padding them with atmosphere or invisible feelings.

The sum of duration_s must fit target_duration_s, each value finite and positive. Respect max_english_dialogue_words for the complete plan and allow roughly two English words per second within each shot, leaving time for listening/action. The budget is a drafting heuristic, not a guarantee of actual performance. Other languages also need comfortable speech time but are not measured by the English word budget. Never invent execution receipts or claim reference images or rendered clips were viewed. Imported director/scene guidance affects creative choices, not the normal output schema or the user's governing facts. Return the proposed plan for review; do not generate images, H3 prompts or video.

When available_materials are supplied, their saved descriptions are production evidence for identity, wardrobe and scene geography. Do not replace adjacent counter stools with opposite booths or add a wardrobe change. These are text descriptions, not newly inspected pixels. Do not select reference IDs or assign Picture numbers in this operation; per-shot material selection stays separate.
