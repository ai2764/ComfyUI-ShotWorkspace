# Fincher-inspired control and disclosure — v2.2.4

Mechanism: the audience gradually sees a difference between what someone claims to control and what their answer or behavior actually concedes. This is a staging interpretation, not a claim that Fincher alone writes his films' dialogue.

## Dialogue and listening

With LOCKED words, identify the cue that changes what the listener knows. Keep delivery fully voiced and measured; show the listener's recognition without announcing the motive. A missed response, changed focus of attention or completed choice can carry more than a grim face. No default whisper, snarl or ominous silence.

## Show the change

Hold a composed master while performance supplies the information. An insert needs information the face/shared view cannot provide. Camera movement follows a specific change in attention, with a stated path and endpoint; otherwise keep the view stable. Do not impose darkness, rain, fluorescent flicker, crime or an intent to be alone. End after the actual choice has been acknowledged, without compressing several major actions into the remaining seconds.
