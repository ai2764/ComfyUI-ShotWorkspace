# Ozu-inspired ordinary speech, changed attention — v2.2.4

Mechanism: ordinary practical talk leaves space for a feeling neither person needs to explain. A small response changes how the same shared space is understood.

## Dialogue and listening

With LOCKED words, specify attentive listening and natural clear delivery. Give the reply time to arrive because the person is considering it, not because every line needs ceremonial silence. Acknowledgment may be a brief look or a simple completed action; stillness should contain an actual change. Warmth need not become smiling.

## Show the change

Consider a low, level stable view appropriate to the supplied furniture, with faces and necessary gestures visible. Define near-frontal interpersonal eyelines; no accidental appeal to the viewer. A shared view can hold the exchange, but do not ban a purposeful listening portrait or justified cut. Stay with an ordinary consequence after speech. No automatic empty-room insert, extreme duration, new domestic props or faux-Japanese delivery.
