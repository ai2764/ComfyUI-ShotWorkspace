# Provenance and limits — 2.2.4

These are original selective technique profiles. They are neither official filmmaker skills nor comprehensive descriptions of a career. Dialogue, story, performance and visual choices in films belong to multiple collaborators. The mechanisms and practical micro-directions in this package are our interpretation and must be tested, not attributed verbatim to interviewees. No movie dialogue, article text or footage is bundled.

Primary/context sources checked 2026-11-10:

- [Tod Lippy interviews Wes Anderson](https://www.todlippy.com/writing/interviews/an-interview-with-wes-anderson): discussion of writing with Owen Wilson, planning staging, and shared frames that show several actors' reactions. Our formal-vulnerability dialogue mechanism is an authored interpretation.
- [Billy Wilder interviewed by James Linville, The Paris Review](https://www.theparisreview.org/interviews/1432/the-art-of-screenwriting-no-1-billy-wilder): accessible opening discusses collaboration and concrete dramatic expression. The later subscriber section was not accessed. Our verbal-turn recipe is not presented as a quoted Wilder rule.
- [Joel and Ethan Coen, Walker Art Center Regis Dialogue transcript, 2009](https://s3.amazonaws.com/wac-imgix/cms/Coens-Dialogue-Formatted.pdf): direct conversation about collaboration and production. It is contextual provenance, not evidence for every comic device in the authored profile.
- [DGA Quarterly, Playing With a New Deck](https://www.dga.org/craft/dgaq/issues/1301-winter-2013/house-of-cards): a production-specific account of performance-led master shots and motivated camera movement. This does not make a collaborator's screenplay voice Fincher's sole authorship.
- [Criterion, The Ozu Shot](https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/2257-the-ozu-shot-tokyo-ga-and-late-spring): cinematographer Yuharu Atsuta on low, still framing. The practical-talk and listening directions are our interpretation, not claims made by this short source.

Sources/examples/evaluation logs are not loaded on every model call. Runtime uses one compact injection packet OR shared guidance plus one profile. Rendered acting, timing, intelligibility and recognizable resemblance remain unverified until actual footage is reviewed.
